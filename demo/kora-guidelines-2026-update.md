# Kora Naturals — Guidelines Update: Social & Spelling (October 2026)

> Fictional demo document. Import this into Plumb's Policy Studio to watch prose become checkable rules.

## 8. Social media

8.1 Every Instagram post must include our brand hashtag #KoraNaturals so community posts stay discoverable.

8.2 Never mention competitor brands by name in our content. In particular, do not name GlowCo or PureLeaf; compare against "other gels" if you must compare at all.

8.3 Do not use the phrase "holy grail" to describe any product. It is overused and sounds like hype.

## 9. Spelling

9.1 We write in Indian English. Use "colour" not "color", "moisturise" not "moisturize", and "moisturiser" not "moisturizer".

## 10. Tone on social

10.1 Keep social captions calm. Avoid hype words such as "insane", "crazy" and "mind-blowing" — describe the actual benefit instead.
